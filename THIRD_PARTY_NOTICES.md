# Sources and third-party notices

The frozen cohorts and captured evidence use development examples from [HotpotQA, Yang et al.](https://hotpotqa.github.io/) and [MuSiQue, Trivedi et al.](https://github.com/StonyBrookNLP/musique). The source benchmarks and the portions of derived artifacts attributable to them retain their respective terms. These artifacts are filtered subsets with local document identifiers, sentence offsets, provenance mappings, compiled retrieval material and experiment annotations. Each query or label file identifies its dataset. In the mixed corpus and compiled files, use document IDs in the per-dataset query candidates to trace the corresponding records.

- HotpotQA dataset and processed Wikipedia text: [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). The distractor development split was read from the [pinned Hugging Face dataset revision](https://huggingface.co/datasets/hotpotqa/hotpot_qa/tree/1908d6afbbead072334abe2965f91bd2709910ab/distractor).
- MuSiQue dataset, official evaluator and the vendored evaluator's `metrics` dependencies: [upstream CC BY 4.0 license](https://github.com/StonyBrookNLP/musique/blob/922ac98f19a201998dbdae6d7f2887a5258dbdeb/LICENSE). The evaluator is stored under a local filename in the experiment directories. Source paths and revision are recorded in [topic2/sources.json](topic2/sources.json).
